Face detection: OpenCV Zoo YuNet, face_detection_yunet_2023mar.onnx (MIT).
https://github.com/opencv/opencv_zoo/tree/main/models/face_detection_yunet

Face recognition: InsightFace ArcFace w600k_r50.onnx, ResNet50@WebFace600K.
Pretrained InsightFace recognition weights are provided for non-commercial research.
https://github.com/deepinsight/insightface/tree/master/python-package

Inference: Microsoft ONNX Runtime (MIT).
https://github.com/microsoft/onnxruntime

The original model checksums and the detector shape-only conversion are recorded in models/info.json.
